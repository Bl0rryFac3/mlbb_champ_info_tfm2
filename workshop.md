Adds passive and basic attack cards to Champ Info. Heroes with both descriptions show five cards: passive, basic attack, Skill 1, Skill 2 and Ultimate.

[h1] v0.2.4 [/h1]
Includes both icons for the 26-hero MLBB Heroes v2.7.0 roster. This update adds Eudora, Julian, Popol & Kupa, Granger, Hanzo and Atlas. Card descriptions and ability names follow the installed hero mod.

[h1] Compatibility [/h1]
Requires Teamfight Manager 2 0.7.3 or newer. This mod overrides the Champ Info layout. Use one Champ Info layout mod at a time.

The original basic attack card mod is by shirograhm. The passive card and MLBB icons are maintained by Bl0rryFac3.
